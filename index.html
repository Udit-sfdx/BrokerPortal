Seven AI agents do the busy work

A person approves every action that reaches a client, a regulator or money. Each agent works only with the asking user's own firm data.

Lead Qualifier

Scores each new lead with its reasons and fills qualification fields from the conversation.

Follow-up Writer

Drafts replies in the client's language from approved templates. Never sends by itself.

Deal Coach

Flags stuck and blocked deals and names the missing step.

Document Reader

Reads passports, Emirates IDs and licences into fields; a person confirms each one.

Compliance Checker

Screens names, spots large cash payments and drafts the report for the AML officer.

Marketing Analyst

Explains cost per lead and ROI, and drafts ad copy that carries the permit number.

Firm Copilot

Answers questions and runs actions across the portal, and asks before any change.
